Order this

Croissants, breads, pastries, coffee and more. Ranked by what 464 Google reviewers keep coming back for.

  1. Almond croissant

    Dense and packed with filling. One regular says they’ve tried every almond croissant in Philly, and since this one, none of the others come close.

  2. Chocolate croissant $4 · per a review

    The lamination people talk about: buttery layers that stay soft and chewy inside.

  3. Vietnamese iced coffee

    Amanda’s own secret recipe. More than one reviewer calls it the best they’ve had.

  4. Sandwiches on house bread

    Breakfast and lunch on bread from the same ovens. Reviewers say they never miss.

  5. Cream puffs

    Split, filled with cream and dusted with sugar. Straight from their own Instagram.

Andre & Amanda

James Beard Foundation Awards, 2016. Semifinalists: Andre Chin and Amanda Eap.

The bakers behind the counter

It’s a family-run bakery. Reviewers single out the owner-baker’s hand with laminated dough, and say Amanda makes nearly everything herself, from the croissants to the cronuts to the iced coffee. Several call her an absolute gem.

Their own caption on the certificate: still no win, but still an award.
