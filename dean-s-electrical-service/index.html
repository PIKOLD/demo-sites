Who we are:

Dean and David run the business themselves, and they're involved in every part of it.

Between them, more than 45 years of electrical experience. The goal hasn't changed: repeat customers and referrals, on a business built on integrity and value.

Our start

Dean's Electrical Service opened its doors in 2002.

Since then it has grown across Tampa Bay and the communities around it, working alongside many of Tampa's home design firms. The crew has years of on-the-job experience and keeps up with what's new in the trade.
