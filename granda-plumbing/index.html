Chapter one · The work

What we get called for.

Residential plumbing first, light commercial too. These are the jobs our customers keep writing about.

Tankless water heaters

No hot water.

When a tankless heater quits, the whole house feels it: no hot shower, no dishes, no laundry. One family's went out on a Tuesday, and their usual plumber couldn't come until the end of the week. A friend sent them to Granda instead.

Calling for a parent?

Mention that the heater is tankless. It's the first thing a plumber will want to know.

Pinhole leaks · copper pipe

A leak inside the wall.

Pinhole leaks in copper pipe hide behind drywall until a stain shows up. For one master bathroom, Kim in our office laid out exactly what the job involved and coordinated the repair from first call to finish.

Who picks up

Kim is the office manager and job coordinator, by a reviewer's account the person who keeps a job clear.

Repairs

Valves, fixtures, the everyday.

A shut-off that won't shut, a valve that's given up. On one call, Manny and his partner arrived early, found the faulty valve and had it repaired quickly and cleanly.

Emergencies

Emergency plumbing service is available. Call the shop and say so up front.

Chapter three · Booking for someone else

Booking a plumber for Mom or Dad?

Tell us whose home it is, where, who will open the door and what went wrong. You get a short script to read on the phone and a text that's ready to send.

Why a script

Calling on someone else's behalf, it's easy to forget the one detail that matters. Read it straight off the screen.
