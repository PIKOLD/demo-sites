FAQ, straight

Honestly,

Seven questions people ask before they let anyone near their panel. Anything else: (720) 213-8113.

Do you charge by the hour?

Honestly, no. Jobs are priced by the service, so the bill doesn't grow with the clock.

Will I know the price before you start?

Honestly, yes. Costs are shared before any work is done. No surprise bill at the end.

Is the electrician paid to sell me more?

Honestly, no. Electricians are on salary. Nobody earns a commission on an upsell.

Will I know when someone's coming?

Honestly, yes. You get a text when your technician is on the way.

Is the work covered?

Honestly, for two years. The warranty covers labor and the parts we supply.

Can I pay by card?

Honestly, any card. All credit and debit cards, at no extra charge.

When can I reach you?

Honestly, weekdays. Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
