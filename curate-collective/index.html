Word from
the floor

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: a beautiful store, friendly staff who seem genuinely excited to help, and a selection you won't find in big-box stores.

  1. Fair offers on your pairs. People who brought sneakers in to sell or trade say the price was fair — with store credit as an option.
  2. They'll build the fit. The staff put together a whole birthday outfit for one shopper, who got compliments on it all night.
  3. A real eye for it. Shoes and vintage come home in excellent shape. Regulars mention Clayton and Eric by name.
