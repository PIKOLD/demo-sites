Qualifications

Well-cared-for trees add real value to a property, so who looks after them matters. At Lakeway Tree Experts there’s no contracted salesman: you deal directly with Arlen Fisher, who owns and runs the company.

Arlen opened Midland Tree Service in 1985 as the Permian Basin’s first ISA Certified Arborist, added Angelo Tree Experts in 2007, and came to Austin for the hills and the lakes.
