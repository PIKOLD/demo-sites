Step 01 · Inspection

Someone climbs up before anyone files anything.

It starts on the roof, not on hold with your insurer. We walk it, photograph what wind, hail or plain age has done, then sit down with you and explain it in plain English. Slowly, if this is your first claim.

  • We doWalk the roof, document the damage, and tell you straight what we found.
  • You getPhotos and findings you understand before you decide anything.

Step 02 · Adjuster

When the adjuster comes, we're on the roof too.

Your insurance company sends an adjuster to look at the damage. Your Roofing XL inspector meets them on site, shows them the same spots we documented, and goes over the findings together, so nothing gets lost between two ladders.

  • We doMeet the adjuster on site and walk through our findings.
  • You getUpdates after the visit, not silence and guesswork.
