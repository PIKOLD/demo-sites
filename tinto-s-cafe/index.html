From these mountains to your cup.

Three shipments from Santander so far — and the last post said they were almost out again.

In Colombia, a tinto is the small black coffee you drink all day. At Tinto’s it starts much higher up: shade-grown Castillo, fermented slowly and dried for weeks before it travels to Houston.

The owners say they chose it to represent quality, origin and Colombia — a coffee they’d be proud to pour every single day.
