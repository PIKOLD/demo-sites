02 · Services

Masked off. Fixed. Then painted.

Paint is one line on the estimate. The rest is carpentry and prep, and every part of it is listed on its own so you can see what you are paying for.

01

Exterior painting

The house and the structures behind it, in one scope. One reviewer's job covered the home, three outbuildings, several trim repairs and a refinished front door.

02

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors, cut in clean where one surface meets the next.

03

Siding repair & replacement

A few soft boards or whole runs. Reviewers had large sections replaced before the full exterior was painted.

04

Wood rot & trim

Rotted wood cut out and rebuilt, so the paint has something sound to hold.

05

Deck repairs, stain & sealer

Damaged boards repaired first, then the deck stained and sealed.

06

Popcorn ceiling removal

Textured ceilings taken down to a flat, smooth finish.

07

Fences

Wood fence installation and repairs, then stain and sealer.

08

Kitchen cabinet painting

Doors and boxes painted for a new-looking kitchen without new cabinets.
