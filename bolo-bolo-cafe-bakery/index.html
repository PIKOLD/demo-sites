What’s on the plates

Baked here, changed with the seasons

The counter started as a home bakery and still bakes like one: small batches that follow the season. Recent ones include a chai olive oil cake, cardamom buns and a mandarin orange black sesame cookie.

The pastry case is small and rotates — come early for the best pick.

One reviewer came for the specials and went back to edit the review just to add the chai olive oil cake.

How it started

From Isha’s home bakery to a corner of Court Street

Isha grew up in Jaipur. Bolo Bolo is her love letter to it — color, craft and community, served on a small round table.

The room is a collection of little details: hand-carved menu boards, block-print tiles, decor that’s largely thrifted and cutlery worth noticing. It was designed with two local creatives, and it doubles as a neighborhood gathering place.

What people say

Small room, big following on Court Street

4.8★★★★★
368 Google reviews

  1. A barista who teaches as he pours

    Guests describe a friendly, knowledgeable barista who walks each customer through their drink — people leave happier and know more about what’s in the cup.

  2. Specials that aren’t too sweet

    The mango lassi matcha and matcha mahal get called delicious and refreshing, and the chai olive oil cake is singled out as amazing. The iced coffee is praised as smooth and well brewed.

  3. Tiny inside, lovely outside

    Indoor seating is a squeeze, reviewers admit — but the outdoor setup gets top marks for fresh air and prime people-watching in Cobble Hill.
