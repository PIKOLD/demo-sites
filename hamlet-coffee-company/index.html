A guest shelf that changes.

hamlet brings in coffee from roasters around the world — Oslo, Stockholm, Seoul, Tallinn, Portland, Dallas, the UK — and introduces each one as it lands. Regulars come by on Saturday mornings to taste whoever is new, and leave with a few new favorites.

At the bar, the menu stays short and solid: espresso, cortados, lattes, and a hot chocolate for the kids.

641.877 · Seasonal boardCard 2 of 4

The board turns with the weather.

Fall means a homemade Pumpkin Spice, Cardamom and Maple lattes, Chaider and Apple Cider. Spring brings Lavender, Ube and Strawberry Matcha. The Ube Latte has a habit of staying on by popular demand.

To go with it: a maple cruller, or a blueberry lemon scone from Runner & Stone.

Brick, maple, and room to sit.

A calm, minimal room with plenty of seating — good for breakfast, lunch or an hour of work. There's an outdoor courtyard for warm days, and yes, there's a restroom.

Weekend mornings get busy. Come early if you want a table.
