What regulars say

A Little Tokyo stop people grew up with

  • So much variety: little pastries, breads, cake slices and whole cakes, and if something is out they will often make it on the spot.
  • The strawberry roll and the steamed buns are the two things people keep telling friends about.
  • Prices stay friendly. More than one review says it has been around longer than they have.
