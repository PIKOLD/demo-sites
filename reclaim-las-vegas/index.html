The store

One roof, 20+ racks of other people's taste

Reclaim is a vintage collective: every rack belongs to a different Las Vegas seller, so one visit covers a dozen styles. Two locations of the night market, two Fridays a month through the winter.

  • True vintage → Y2K

    Pieces from the 50s through the 80s, Y2K, and modern trends — curated by 20+ independent vendors, not one buyer.

  • Accessories

    Purses, shoes, hats, belts and designer finds.

  • Try it on

    There's a dressing room — staff will point you to it.

  • Easy to stop by

    Downtown in the Arts District, with parking right out front.

  • Night Market

    Friday nights: dozens of sellers, good eats, good music — and live art at the Arts District nights.
