The family behind the counter

a mother,a daughter,a son.

Visitors in town for a few days end up coming back visit after visit, and they talk about the same three people: friendly, quick, and working hard even when only one or two of them are on shift.

The young man at the bar does not hold back on the caramel drizzle. The woman at the register takes time to explain how the date seed coffee is made. Happy Halloween, they signed one post: from the Bohemian family.

Work here

A genuine workspace.

That’s their own word for it. Bring the laptop, order a chai, and stay a while. Guests describe the room as relaxed and calm, a different world from the street outside.
