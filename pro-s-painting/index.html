No. 02ResidentialWeek one

Everything is still someone else’s color.

One family who had just moved to Texas put it plainly in their review: they loved the new house, it just didn’t look like them yet. That gap is the job. Pro’s Painting handles the inside, the trim and the brick with one crew, so you aren’t lining up three contractors in your first month.

  1. Painting the house

    Room by room or all at once — the colors that make it look like you actually live there.

  2. Trim, replaced — not just painted over

    Worn or damaged trim comes out, new trim goes in, and it gets finished along with everything else.

  3. Brick repair

    Damaged brick fixed by the same crew that paints, as reviewers describe it: painting, new trim and brick in one job.

  4. Remodels & getting ready to sell

    Quick to come out mid-remodel. One seller says the house was market-ready a day sooner than promised.

No. 03CommercialMeanwhile, at work

At the business, the walls go up before the paint.

Pro’s Painting takes on commercial work too — painting, framing and more. Same crew, same flexible scheduling reviewers keep mentioning, so the job can be planned around the hours your place is busy.

  1. Commercial painting

    For the space you run a business from, scheduled with you rather than dropped on you.

  2. Framing

    New walls framed, then finished and painted by the same people — one phone number from studs to last coat.

  3. Remodel work

    Reviewers mention a quick response, competitive prices and painting that holds up on a remodel.
