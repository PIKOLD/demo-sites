Yesenia

founder · Fulton St, Brooklyn

it all started with a $5 mason jar

A love of baking passed down from her mom, fierce pride in her Dominican heritage, and no business plan — just an apartment kitchen and a lot of hard work.

  1. 2015Yesenia starts baking tres leches at home.
  2. $5The first cakes go out in mason jars.
  3. 2018It officially becomes Sweets & Things — later a pink storefront on Fulton St.
  4. 85+Flavors and counting, rotating every month.
  5. GMAFeatured on Good Morning America, talking about her pumpkin spice tres leches.
