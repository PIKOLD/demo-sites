Every surface, by letter

The letters match the drawing on the cover. Soft wash with biodegradable cleansers where pressure would do harm; high pressure where concrete needs it.

  • AHouse siding

    Soft washed to lift mold, mildew and dirt off the walls.

  • BSoffit, fascia & gutters

    Washed together with window frames and the front entry.

  • CDriveway & sidewalks

    High-pressure flatwork, with pre- and post-treatment that kills algae and fungus at the root.

  • DPatio & entry areas

    Front and back entries brightened. Furniture comes off first.

  • EConcrete & brick

    Dirt and grime pulled out, which helps against seasonal cracking and chipping.

  • FDeck

    Far quicker and more even than scrubbing boards by hand.

  • GFence

    Both faces, post to post.

  • HStorefront & building

    Business exteriors and industrial complexes.

  • IFleet

    Fleet washing for company vehicles.
