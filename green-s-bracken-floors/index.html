The family behind the counter

Owner-operated and family-run, with more than 35 years in the flooring business behind it.

Green's Bracken Floors sells and installs carpet, hardwood, tile, travertine and laminate for homes and businesses, and takes on the less glamorous jobs too: repairs, refinishing, flood remediation, project management.

In the reviews, Lauren and her dad come up by name, along with Mike and Chris, usually next to words like patient, responsive and prompt.
