What travelers write home about

  1. Drinks that carry a passport

    Guests love that the owner builds each drink from a place she has traveled, with a little flag and toppings to match. The Tokyo matcha gets called the best matcha they've had.

  2. An owner who makes you feel expected

    Reviewers describe her and the barista as warm, knowledgeable and genuinely kind, the kind of welcome people say they haven't found elsewhere.

  3. A treasure cove around the counter

    The café sits inside a gift shop and art gallery, so the wait becomes a browse. Small room, a couple of tables, and one guest's to-go cup was carried out to him.
