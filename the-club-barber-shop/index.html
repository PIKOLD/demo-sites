A calm first cut

The shop that made the difference

One of the reviews is from a parent. Their son is autistic, and finding a place where he feels comfortable getting a haircut had always been the hard part — so for years the haircuts happened at home, with a parent who freely admits they're no barber.

Then they came to The Club. In their words, they finally found the barber shop that made all the difference for their son. It sits next to other reviews that describe the room the same way: professional and inviting, clean and well organized, with barbers who are friendly to everyone who walks in.

Nobody needs to rush here. If your kid — or you — needs things a little slower, just say so before you come in.
