Before you order

The lowdown...

How the shop runs, straight from the counter. It's a big part of why dogs leave relaxed.

  1. 3–4 hrs

    A groom takes the morning

    Grooming usually takes 3 to 4 hours. There's no rush service, so plan the day around drop-off and pickup.

  2. Extra time

    Seniors & puppies

    Older dogs and little ones may need additional time. Nobody gets hurried.

  3. Dog-friendly

    Who the salon is for

    The dog crew here are dogs that get along with other dogs and are fine with grooming. It isn't the right room for reactive dogs.

  4. Outside

    Nail trims at the gate

    Got a reactive dog? Nail trims can be done outside, in the gated entrance.

  5. 15+ yrs

    Small & family-operated

    More than 15 years of grooming experience, and an established salon on North Loop since 2017.

  6. N. Loop

    In the heart of the neighborhood

    Just off the Loop, next door to shops with an eclectic vibe — and a lobby that recently got bigger.
